Tick to Pixel a real-time trading desk in .NET MAUI

A cross-platform desktop app in C# that follows every market price from the moment it arrives (the tick) to the moment it's drawn on screen (the pixel), and measures each step on the way. Watchlists, charts and orders sit side by side in one workspace. Paper trading only: it never sends real orders.

See the ideaWhat's coming

The idea: one workspace, many panels

Not a pop-up window for every view. One desk, laid out the way you trade, with as many charts and tickers as you need.

Panels, not pop-ups

Watchlists, charts, time and sales, an order ticket and your positions share one window. Add another chart on another stock. Save a layout and it opens the same way tomorrow. Any panel can still pop out onto a second screen.

Linked by colour

Give panels a colour. Pick a stock in a blue watchlist and every blue panel switches to it, in the same frame. A red chart keeps its own stock. Panels never call each other: one store holds the links.

Fast, and measured

Prices arrive thousands of times a second. The screen redraws only what changed, once per frame. Every claim in the series comes with a measurement and its raw data.

Already working

Built and tested on Windows 11, in a private repository for now. The code is published with the series.

  • BuiltA simulated market you can replayThe same seed gives the same prices, trades and bursts every time, so every test and every video can be repeated.
  • BuiltA live watchlist that keeps upOnce per frame it updates only the rows on screen that changed. Measured: 60 frames a second while 50,000 simulated price updates arrive every second (my Windows 11 laptop).
  • BuiltCandles, time and sales, and my own chartBuilt from every trade, nothing skipped, and drawn on the GPU with SkiaSharp: candles, volume, crosshair, pan and zoom.
  • BuiltViews linked by colour, through a storeOne Fluxor store, used only on the UI thread. Three views on one stock share one market-data subscription.
  • BuiltThe workspace of panelsWatchlists, charts, time and sales, an order ticket and your positions side by side; resizable columns; named layouts saved and restored; any panel pops out into its own window.
  • BuiltPaper tradingOne engine thread owns the money: market and limit orders, cash held for buys, no short selling, and one order per click even on a double click. Measured: an order shows as filled on screen within 3 ms (p99).
  • Built"The store buys atomicity"Cash and shares sent to the screen as two separate updates: about 1 frame in 5 showed numbers that never existed together. Sent as one snapshot through the store: none.

Coming next

  • PlannedCross-platform builds.NET MAUI targets Windows, Mac, Android and iOS. Windows comes first and is the only platform tested so far; a Mac build in CI is next, and phones would need their own layout.
